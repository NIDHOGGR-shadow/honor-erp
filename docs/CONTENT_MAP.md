## US source

The user requested UK and US material. The official [HONOR country/region selector](https://www.honor.com/global/choose-country-region/) retrieved on 6 November 2026 does not list a US regional entry. An official US storefront has not been verified. Keep that source unresolved rather than substituting a different market without telling the user.

## Exact-model check

For example, the official [China HONOR 400 page](https://www.honor.com/cn/phones/honor-400/) and [Nigeria HONOR 400 page](https://www.honor.com/ng/phones/honor-400/) describe different battery/charging specifications. A shared marketing name is insufficient evidence for copying all product facts between regions.

The new product editor records a regional market and optional source link. Source links are reference material; they do not confirm Nigerian stock or imply authorisation to sell a model. Product images should come from the supplier's approved assets or the matching official reference.
